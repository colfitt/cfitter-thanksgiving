Gluten free and dairy free: Deegs, veggie plate if the dip is dairy free, green bean salad. Substitutions on dairy can be decided on day of.

Vegetarian: everything except the turkey, turkey gravy and salami. The mushroom gravy is made with mushroom stock.

Nutless. (Except for the cashew dressing on the kale salad)
